01 / CORE IDEA

Why Minimax works here

Tic-Tac-Toe is deterministic, turn-based and fully observable. Because its search space is small, the AI can examine future responses instead of relying on training data.

MAX↕MIN→UTILITY
02 / UTILITY

How outcomes are scored

+10AI WIN
0DRAW
−10AI LOSS

The engine also adjusts terminal scores by depth so a faster win is preferred and a delayed loss is preferred over an immediate loss.

03 / COMPLEXITY

Search cost

O(bd)

For a general game tree, b is the branching factor and d is search depth. Tic-Tac-Toe remains small enough for exhaustive search.
